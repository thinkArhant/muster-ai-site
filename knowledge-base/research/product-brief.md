## Founder's Idea

The founder supplied a complete, settled product spec rather than a raw hypothesis. It is
**`knowledge-base/product-spec-seed.md`** (committed at project start, commit `b265925` — *"founder:
spec seeded — team builds from a settled brief"*). Read that file in full; it is the authoritative
statement of the founder's intent and is not summarized here in a lossy form.

**Linked materials:**
- `knowledge-base/product-spec-seed.md` — the settled spec (page structure, non-negotiable rules,
  locked design direction, measured data, sequencing).
- `github.com/thinkArhant/muster-ai` — the product the site is about.
- `bodh.day` — the shipped case-study artifact referenced throughout the spec.

**What the seed establishes (orientation only — the seed governs):**

- **Product**: the public one-page website for Muster, the open-source multi-agent product framework
  for Claude Code. It shows what Muster is, how it works, and proves with measured data and real
  build artifacts that one person plus a governed AI team ships real products. It ends in a single
  `curl`.
- **Audience**: founders and builders evaluating Muster — designed for the skeptical technical cold
  reader who gives it a 5-second skim.
- **The bar**: the site is itself an exhibit of what Muster produces. Built *with* Muster, by
  Muster's AI team, and says so — its own repo, queue, handoffs, decision log, and build telemetry
  are public. A sloppy page refutes the product.
- **Structure**: 6 sections — hero · the Bodh Sprint-4 replay/case-study (build first) · the insight
  · the four decisions (founder-supplied first-person copy, centerpiece) · shipped with Muster ·
  get started.
- **Stance**: measured numbers stated exactly, never rounded; no invented values (dashes until
  measured); "measured — method and data published," never "proven"; one CTA.

**Nature of this brief**: the founder's framing is *"the thinking is done; the job is executing it
excellently."* This is an execution brief, not a hypothesis awaiting market validation.
